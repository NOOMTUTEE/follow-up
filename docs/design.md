## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an executive-tier command environment tailored for high-velocity relationship management and pipeline governance. The brand personality is authoritative, sharp, unhurried, and exceptionally disciplined. Rather than overwhelming operators with excessive visual clutter, the interface uses deliberate spatial cadence and architectural balance to promote focus and confidence.

The design movement bridges Modern Corporate Precision with subtle Tonal Minimalism:
- **Structural Integrity:** Crisp planar geometry where data density does not compromise visual breathing room.
- **Controlled Hierarchy:** Information surfaces lean into layered micro-elevations, fine-hairline structural delimiters, and deep slate typography to convey rigor.
- **Target Audience:** Account directors, enterprise sales leads, and operations managers who handle dozens of follow-ups daily and require zero cognitive friction between review and action.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-trust, low-fatigue workspace optimized for sustained daytime readability and clear scan paths.

### Core Roles & Rationale
- **Primary (`#1E40AF` - Slate Indigo):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, active sidebar states, focused selection bounds, and dominant brand anchors. Communicates institutional weight and precision.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9` - Sky Cyan):** Acts as an informative accent for fresh records, active discovery indicators, and newly captured lead signals.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald):** Reserved for resolution metrics, closed-won statuses, completed tasks, and positive velocity indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** Grounds the typographical foundation, ensuring absolute contrast without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Surface & Border Architecture
- **Application Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` provides a cool, low-glare backdrop separating structural cards from screen edges.
- **Elevated Canvas & Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` pure white surfaces frame data modules and interactive sheets.
- **Structural Delimiters:** `#E2E8F0` defines 1px boundaries, subtle dividers, and table row partitions.
- **Subtle Fill / Hover:** `#F1F5F9` powers muted chips, neutral badge containers, and hover states.

### Status Semantic System
- **New / Lead:** Fill `#F0F9FF`, Border `#BAE6FD`, Text `#0284C7`.
- **In Progress / Contacted:** Fill `#FFFBEB`, Border `#FDE68A`, Text `#D97706`.
- **Needs Attention / Urgent:** Fill `#FEF2F2`, Border `#FECACA`, Text `#DC2626`.
- **Completed / Closed:** Fill `#ECFDF5`, Border `#A7F3D0`, Text `#059669`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers with **Manrope** for analytical and data-heavy interface context. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides refined geometric weight with clean apertures, instilling immediate authority across screen summaries and section leads.
- **Body & Controls:** Manrope offers tall x-height and open letterforms, preventing eye strain during deep table audits and activity logging.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All currency, contact counter, date, and metrics data apply OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to maintain vertical column alignment across rows and summary panels.
- **Micro-Labels:** Header tags, column sorting titles, and secondary metadata use uppercase tracking (+0.04em) at 11px and 12px for crisp legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a strict 8px structural module (with 4px sub-increments for fine component alignment).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a fixed 260px primary navigation rail. Page container max-width: 1600px with 32px (`margin`) margins and 24px (`gutter`) column spacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The navigation rail collapses into an icon-only dock (72px). Gutters shift to 16px (`gutter-sm`), canvas margins reduce to 24px.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stack flow. 16px (`margin-mobile`) horizontal canvas margin. Global navigation shifts to a persistent top bar with slide-out sheet drawer.

### Spacing Distribution Rules
- Components leverage `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled interior elements (icon-to-label, badge paddings).
- Form inputs, table cells, and standard control heights employ `space-md` (16px) for spacious vertical ease.
- Major card groupings and modular board columns employ `space-lg` (24px) for distinct territorial demarcation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary outlines combined with soft ambient light falloffs, avoiding synthetic layered clutter.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background layer (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow. Holds primary layout slots and Kanban channel tracks.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), supported by subtle diffused light: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Rows):** Elevated state during drag or hover: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdown Menus & Popovers):** Positioned utility layers: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` with `#E2E8F0` hairline envelope.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Command Palettes):** Centered viewports over a dimmed scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` backdrop with 4px backdrop blur). Elevation: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16), 0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2** (0.5rem / 8px baseline radius), delivering a clean, tailored contour that reads as modern without drifting into casual playfulness.

### Scale Application
- **Default (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to primary buttons, input fields, select dropdowns, search triggers, metric modules, and table container enclosures.
- **Sub-elements (6px / `0.375rem`):** Applied to internal items such as table action buttons, segmented controls, and nested list items.
- **Large (16px / `1rem`):** Structural modals, fly-out details drawers, and multi-stage workflow cards.
- **Pill (9999px):** Strictly reserved for status badges, counter pills, and presence indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E40AF` fill with `#FFFFFF` label. Height: 40px (desktop), padding: 0 16px. Border radius: 8px. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E3A8A`. Focus ring: 3px offset `#93C5FD`.
- **Secondary:** White `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` label. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background and `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Destructive:** Soft red `#FEF2F2` background with `#DC2626` text and border. Hover: `#FEE2E2`.
- **Icon Buttons:** 36x36px square with 8px radius. Neutral `#475569` text, background transparent, hover fill `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Dimensions:** Height 24px, horizontal padding 8px, fully rounded (pill shape).
- **Typography:** `label-sm` (11px, 600 weight, uppercase tracking).
- **Structure:** 1px border matched to semantic state with translucent fill (e.g., Follow-up Pending uses `#FEF2F2` background, `#FECACA` border, `#DC2626` text).

### Form Inputs & Selects
- **Height & Bounds:** 40px standard input field with 8px border radius. 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Background & Text:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#0F172A` value text, `#94A3B8` placeholder.
- **Focus Experience:** Smooth 150ms transition to 1px `#1E40AF` border paired with an external 3px glow ring in `rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.15)`. No default browser outline.
- **Labeling:** Top-aligned 13px medium (`#475569`) with 6px vertical clearance from field border.

### Data Tables (CRM Contact List)
- **Header:** Height 44px, background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Typography: `label-sm` in `#475569`.
- **Rows:** Minimum height 52px, zebra striping avoided in favor of clean 1px bottom divider `#F1F5F9`. Hover row highlight: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Numerics & Dates:** Right-aligned or tab-aligned using tabular figures with secondary muted styling (`#64748B`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px, 4px border radius. Default state: 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`, `#FFFFFF` fill. Checked state: `#1E40AF` fill with sharp white check glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18x18px circle with centered 6px dot upon selection. Active ring matches primary focus behavior.

### Cards & Kanban Columns
- **Cards:** Surface white `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 16px internal padding. Left status accent bar (3px width) dynamically styled to lead status.
- **Kanban Board Tracks:** Background `#F1F5F9` with 12px radius, 12px interior padding, header count badge in `#E2E8F0` fill and `#334155` text.

### Activity Timeline Feed
- Continuous 2px vertical guide rail (`#E2E8F0`) with 28px circular status icons indicating call notes, emails, stage transitions, and reminders.
